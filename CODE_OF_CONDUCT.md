# Code of Conduct

This is a project about health, so we hold ourselves to a high standard of care and respect.

## Our standard

- Be respectful and welcoming to everyone, whatever their gender, background, or experience.
- Use inclusive, gender-neutral language (for example "people who menstruate").
- Treat menstrual and reproductive health as a normal, serious topic. No stigma, no jokes at anyone's expense.
- Give and accept feedback graciously. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy. Never share or request someone's personal health information.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, sexualised language or imagery, sharing others' private information, and anything that makes the project unsafe or unwelcoming.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people who break these rules. To report a concern, use GitHub's private reporting on this repository or contact a maintainer through GitHub.
